How to create a Gmail App Password

  1. Open your Google Account → Security and turn on 2-Step Verification (required).
  2. Go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  3. Type a name such as "RemoteApply" and click Create.
  4. Copy the 16-character password Google shows (spaces do not matter) and paste it into the Password field on the Email settings page.

Your normal Google password will not work. If you do not see the App Passwords page, your account may be a managed Workspace account, or 2-Step Verification is off.

Why do some jobs only say "Apply on site"?

Most employers collect applications through their own forms. We can only email a job when its description contains a plausible application address. Jobs without one show Apply on site and open the original posting, and they cannot be added to a bulk email.

Responsible use

  • Send only applications that are relevant to you and the role. Do not mass-mail jobs you would not actually take.
  • You are responsible for every email sent from your account, and for following the email and anti-spam laws that apply to you and to the recipient (for example CAN-SPAM, GDPR/PECR, CASL).
  • Keep the daily limit low. Providers can suspend accounts that send many similar messages quickly.
  • This app has no login. Run it only on your own machine. Do not expose it to the internet.
  • Review every email in the preview. AI-written text is checked against your CV, but you are the one who sends it.
